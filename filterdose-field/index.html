Field-use guidance

Estimates are not final manufacturer-approved dosing. Dimensions estimate only water between media grains and above the bed in the modeled cylinder. Actual water volume can differ due to dished heads, underdrains, media irregularity, and retained water. Whenever possible, use Measured gal or confirm tank capacity with the filter manufacturer before treatment.

Chem-Clean Express sand-filter directions: Backwash and drain; prepare 11 oz cleaner per 5 gal water; add enough prepared solution to cover the sand; keep manway open during soaking for pressure relief; soak at least 1 hour; backwash until discharge runs clear. Follow current label/SDS, isolation procedures, chemical compatibility rules, and facility-specific requirements.
