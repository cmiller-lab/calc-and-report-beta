Interpretation

  • Below -0.3 indicates increasingly corrosive water.
  • Above +0.3 indicates increasing scale-forming tendency.
  • LSI is an equilibrium indicator, not a complete corrosion assessment.

Method

LSI = measured pH − saturation pH. CYA correction uses one-third of measured CYA as an operational approximation.

Hydraulic Selection Method

Hydraulic fit is the primary criterion. The fit score weights operating-speed quality at 50%, scaled-curve position at 35%, and available frequency reserve at 15%.

The final selection score weights hydraulic fit at 85% and the estimated energy score at 15%. This prevents a theoretically lower-energy pump from displacing a substantially better hydraulic selection.

Curve position is a field-selection proxy, not Pentair Best Efficiency Point data. Pentair-certified BHP, pump-efficiency contours, and NPSHr are not included.

Energy Estimate Method

Estimated shaft power = motor nameplate HP × (required Hz ÷ 60)³. Estimated electrical input then applies the user-entered motor and VFD efficiencies.

These values are useful for relative screening only and are not manufacturer-certified electrical-consumption predictions.
